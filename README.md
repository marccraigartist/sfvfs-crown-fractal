# SFVFS Crown Fractal

[Open the interactive website](https://marccraigartist.github.io/sfvfs-crown-fractal/)

A two-dimensional visual study of Marc Craig's SFVFS three-axis gyroscope: A/X opposed crowns, recursive rings, a central hexagon and star projection, moving crowns, and rainbow fans. All three axes begin at B12 and share one twelve-seat clock.

Use the controls to pause, restart at B12, change speed, scrub one cycle, turn the six geometry layers on or off, and zoom into the recursive children. Drag or pinch to explore.

The rainbow fans use eight known zeta-zero heights. Their crown angles can be enlarged up to 20× for display. This is a visualization, not a prime or zero predictor or a proof of the Riemann hypothesis.

The complete program is in [index.html](index.html); it has no external runtime dependencies.
